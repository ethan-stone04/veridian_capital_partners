About the Firm

Veridian Capital Partners is a mid-market corporate finance firm specializing in capital structure advisory, financial planning, and M&A transaction support for industrial and energy sector clients across the Southern United States.

Founded in the aftermath of the 2008 financial crisis, the firm was built on the belief that companies deserve transparent, data-driven financial guidance — not just deal volume. Veridian serves private companies, family-owned businesses, and publicly traded mid-caps looking to optimize their balance sheets, raise debt capital, or pursue strategic acquisitions.

Case Studies

Three representative client stories · Industrial · Energy · Services

Every engagement begins with the same question: what does this company actually need? Below are three representative stories of how Veridian has partnered with mid-market clients across the Southern U.S. — optimizing balance sheets, navigating transitions, and executing strategic transactions.

Case 01

Family-Owned Industrial Manufacturer · Houston, TX

Hartwell Steel & Fabrication

A third-generation fabricator refinances legacy debt and unlocks growth capital

Industrial

The Challenge

Hartwell, a 60-year-old family-owned steel fabricator serving petrochemical and infrastructure clients, was servicing $42M of legacy debt at punitive post-COVID interest rates. The founding family had also been approached by a private equity suitor offering a partial liquidity event — but the term sheet was structured to strip operational control.

The CEO wanted to preserve family ownership while freeing up capital to expand into offshore wind component fabrication.

Veridian's Approach

Rather than defaulting to a sale, Veridian's Treasury and FP&A teams modeled three alternative capital structures. The recommended path: a senior secured refinancing with a regional bank syndicate, paired with a $15M mezzanine tranche from a long-hold credit fund.

The structure lowered blended cost of capital by ~240 bps and preserved 100% family equity, while providing the growth capital Hartwell needed.

Case 02

Publicly Traded Mid-Cap · Oklahoma City, OK

Meridian Basin Resources

A permian basin operator pivots its portfolio through strategic divestiture

Energy

The Challenge

Meridian Basin, a $480M market cap E&P company, had accumulated a portfolio of non-core gas assets in the Anadarko Basin that were dragging on consolidated returns. Activist shareholders were publicly pressuring the board to divest, but prior attempts had stalled due to valuation gaps with potential buyers.

The board needed a partner who could run a disciplined process without leaking information into an already-volatile sector.

Veridian's Approach

The M&A team ran a two-phase process: a targeted outreach to five strategic buyers with geographic overlap, followed by a limited auction for financial sponsors. In parallel, Veridian built an asset-level DCF that reframed the reserves under updated commodity curves — supporting a higher defensible valuation.

The winning bid came in 18% above initial management expectations, and the transaction closed on a compressed 4-month timeline.

Case 03

Private Company · Birmingham, AL

Southern Logistics Group

A regional trucking firm executes a strategic acquisition to enter cold-chain

Services

The Challenge

Southern Logistics, a privately held $95M revenue freight carrier, wanted to acquire a specialty cold-chain operator to diversify beyond commoditized dry van routes. The target — a regional refrigerated carrier with $40M in revenue — had attractive margins but significant customer concentration risk.

Management needed rigorous diligence plus acquisition financing that wouldn't over-lever the combined entity.

Veridian's Approach

Veridian's Corporate Development team coordinated financial, operational, and customer diligence over a 10-week period, identifying a concentrated customer relationship that justified a $4M earnout structure rather than pure cash consideration.

The Treasury team simultaneously sourced an ABL facility plus a seller note, financing the transaction without requiring new equity dilution. Post-close integration tracked to Veridian's synergy model within 90 days.
